# Shows Page Format Guide

Use these rules when updating `/home/runner/work/perceptionzero/perceptionzero/shows.html`:

## Upcoming Shows
- Date format: `Weekday, Month Day, Year`
- Example: `Friday, July 24, 2026`
- Include weekday and year for every upcoming show date.

## Previous Shows
- Date format: `Month Day Year`
- Example: `July 9 2026`
- Do not include weekday in previous show dates.
